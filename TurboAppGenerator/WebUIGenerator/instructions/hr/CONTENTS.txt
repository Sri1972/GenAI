HR DOMAIN — TalentScope
========================

App Name: people-analytics
Theme: Modern HR tech — clean, human-centered, generous spacing
Accent: #6366F1 (indigo — trust, stability)
Secondary: #EC4899 (pink — diversity, warmth)
Style: Light mode, large typography, soft shadows, 16px corners

PAGES:
  1. Org Pulse — Engagement sunburst (radial, NOT a dashboard) + multi-line mood trend
  2. Nine-Box — Interactive 3x3 performance/potential grid with employee chips (NOT a scatter/bubble chart)
  3. 1:1 Tracker — Manager check-in logs with cadence heatmap + sentiment trends
  4. Goal Progress — OKR tracking with radar chart + cumulative progress line chart
  5. HR Copilot — AI assistant for people queries
  6. Workforce Insights — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Sunburst chart (engagement by department/level, Org Pulse)
  - Multi-line chart (mood/sentiment trend by department, Org Pulse)
  - Mini circular gauges (per-employee engagement, Org Pulse flight-risk cards)
  - Interactive 3x3 grid (performance vs potential, employee chips per cell, Nine-Box)
  - Cadence heatmap (1:1 meeting frequency, 1:1 Tracker)
  - Stacked horizontal bar (1:1 mood breakdown per manager, 1:1 Tracker)
  - Radar/spider chart (goal category progress, Goal Progress)
  - Line chart (cumulative goals completed over time, Goal Progress)
  - Radar/spider chart (employee skills assessment — 6 dimensions, Workforce Insights)
  - Sunburst chart (org hierarchy: dept → team → level, Workforce Insights)
  - Box plot (salary distribution by department, Workforce Insights)
  - Funnel chart (hiring pipeline: Applied → Phone → Onsite → Offer → Hired, Workforce Insights)
  - Histogram (engagement score distribution, Workforce Insights)
  - Polar/rose chart (1:1 meeting topics frequency by category, Workforce Insights)

DATA TABLES:
  - employees (demographics, salary, performance, engagement, flight_risk)
  - one_on_ones (meeting logs, mood, topics, sentiment)
  - goals (OKRs — title, category, status, progress, due date)
