HEALTHCARE DOMAIN — MedFlow
============================

App Name: clinical-ops
Theme: Healthcare operations — clean, clinical, high-contrast
Accent: #0891B2 (cyan/teal — clinical calm)
Secondary: #DC2626 (red — critical alerts only)
Style: Light mode, large fonts, dense layouts (designed for charge nurses)

PAGES:
  1. Bed Board — Visual bed grid showing occupancy, acuity, isolation status (spatial, NOT a table)
  2. Patient Journey — Sankey diagram of patient movement + LOS box plot + readmission bars
  3. Shift Handoff — Priority-lane handoff notes with progress ring + nurse workload bar chart
  4. Unit Capacity — Gantt-style stacked-bar capacity forecast timeline (predictive, next 7 days)
  5. Clinical AI — AI assistant for patient queries
  6. Unit Analytics — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Sankey diagram (patient flow: admission source → unit → discharge destination, Patient Journey + Unit Analytics)
  - Violin/box plot (length of stay distribution by unit, Patient Journey + Unit Analytics)
  - Horizontal bar chart (readmission rate by unit, Patient Journey)
  - Bar chart (nurse workload / patients per nurse, Shift Handoff)
  - Progress ring (handoff completeness, Shift Handoff)
  - Gantt-style stacked bar (capacity forecast timeline, Unit Capacity)
  - Gauge chart row (3 gauges, Unit Analytics)
  - Polar/rose chart (admissions by hour of day, Unit Analytics)
  - Funnel chart (patient journey: Admitted → Treatment → Recovery → Discharge, Unit Analytics)
  - Histogram (LOS distribution, Unit Analytics)

DATA TABLES:
  - patients (demographics, unit, acuity, status, LOS, attending)
  - bed_board (bed status, occupancy, isolation, equipment)
  - handoff_notes (shift change summaries, priorities, action items)
