HEALTHCARE DOMAIN — MedFlow
============================

App Name: clinical-ops
Theme: Healthcare operations — clean, clinical, high-contrast
Accent: #0891B2 (cyan/teal — clinical calm)
Secondary: #DC2626 (red — critical alerts only)
Style: Light mode, large fonts, dense layouts (designed for charge nurses)

PAGES:
  1. Bed Board — Visual bed grid showing occupancy, acuity, isolation status
  2. Patient Flow — Sankey diagram of patient movement (Admit→Unit→Discharge)
  3. Handoff — Shift handoff notes with priority indicators
  4. Unit Analytics — Charts page with hospital metrics (see below)
  5. Clinical AI — AI assistant for patient queries

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Sankey diagram (patient flow: admission source → unit → discharge destination)
  - Gauge chart (bed occupancy rate per unit)
  - Stacked bar chart (patients by acuity per unit)
  - Box plot (length of stay distribution by unit)
  - Polar/rose chart (admissions by hour of day)
  - Funnel chart (patient journey: Admitted → Treatment → Recovery → Discharge)
  - Histogram (LOS distribution)
  - Line chart (daily census trend)
  - Donut chart (insurance mix)

DATA TABLES:
  - patients (demographics, unit, acuity, status, LOS, attending)
  - bed_board (bed status, occupancy, isolation, equipment)
  - handoff_notes (shift change summaries, priorities, action items)
