RETAIL DOMAIN — RetailNerve
============================

App Name: store-ops
Theme: Vibrant retail operations — warm, energetic, card-heavy
Accent: #F59E0B (amber — retail energy)
Secondary: #7C3AED (purple — premium/loyalty)
Style: Light mode, bold headers, rounded 12px corners, playful but professional

PAGES:
  1. Live Floor — Real-time store activity with foot traffic heatmap
  2. Hourly Pulse — Time-series charts of traffic/revenue by hour
  3. Promo War Room — Promotion performance with conversion funnels
  4. Store Cards — Store comparison cards with performance badges
  5. Ask RetailBot — AI assistant for store ops queries
  6. Performance Lab — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Funnel chart (conversion: foot traffic → browsing → basket → purchase)
  - Gauge chart (store performance score per location)
  - Polar/rose chart (revenue by hour of day — peak hours visualization)
  - Treemap (revenue by store format by region)
  - Waterfall chart (revenue bridge: base → promo uplift → seasonality → net)
  - Radar chart (store comparison: traffic, conversion, basket, satisfaction, staff)
  - Histogram (basket size distribution)
  - Bubble chart (stores by sqft vs revenue vs satisfaction)
  - Grouped bar chart (traffic vs transactions per store)
  - Line chart (daily revenue trend, week-over-week)
  - US state map (store locations with revenue heatmap)
  - Stacked area chart (revenue by channel: in-store vs online vs pickup)

DATA TABLES:
  - stores (store info, format, sqft, employees, rating)
  - hourly_metrics (foot traffic, transactions, revenue, queue times)
  - promotions (promo campaigns, redemptions, attributed revenue)
