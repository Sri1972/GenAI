FINANCIAL SERVICES DOMAIN — WealthPulse
========================================

App Name: wealth-advisor
Theme: Institutional wealth management, dark premium aesthetic
Accent: #10B981 (emerald green — growth/positive returns)
Dark mode: Yes

PAGES:
  1. Watchlist — Live portfolio feed with position cards, mini sparklines, donut chart
  2. Trade Blotter — Trade activity ledger, daily bar chart (green/red), dense table
  3. Risk Matrix — Bubble chart (volatility vs Sharpe), risk comparison table
  4. Allocation Drift — Tornado/butterfly chart (target vs actual allocation)
  5. AI Strategist — AI chat for portfolio queries
  6. Analytics Lab — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase page (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Donut chart (sector allocation)
  - Bubble chart (risk scatter — volatility vs Sharpe vs portfolio size)
  - Bar chart (trade activity per day)
  - Tornado/butterfly chart (allocation drift)
  - Sparklines (position trend mini-charts)
  - Radar chart (risk profile — Sharpe, Beta, Volatility, Concentration, Drawdown)
  - Candlestick chart (price history for selected ticker)
  - Waterfall chart (P&L attribution — what drove returns)
  - Gauge chart (portfolio health score)
  - Treemap (sector/ticker weight breakdown)

DATA TABLES:
  - positions (client holdings — ticker, asset_class, market_value, gain_loss)
  - trades (trade log — action, ticker, shares, price, date, rationale)
  - risk_metrics (per-client risk — Sharpe, VaR, beta, volatility)
