HR DOMAIN — TalentScope
========================

App Name: people-analytics
Theme: Modern HR tech — clean, human-centered, generous spacing
Accent: #6366F1 (indigo — trust, stability)
Secondary: #EC4899 (pink — diversity, warmth)
Style: Light mode, large typography, soft shadows, 16px corners

PAGES:
  1. Org Pulse — Workforce health dashboard with engagement heatmap
  2. Nine-Box — Performance vs potential scatter/grid
  3. 1:1 Tracker — Manager check-in logs with sentiment trends
  4. Goal Progress — OKR tracking with progress visualization
  5. HR Copilot — AI assistant for people queries
  6. Workforce Insights — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Radar/spider chart (employee skills assessment — 6 dimensions)
  - Sunburst chart (org hierarchy: dept → team → level)
  - Box plot (salary distribution by department)
  - Funnel chart (hiring pipeline: Applied → Phone → Onsite → Offer → Hired)
  - Bubble chart (nine-box: performance vs potential vs headcount)
  - Histogram (engagement score distribution)
  - Polar chart (1:1 meeting topics frequency by category)
  - Treemap (headcount by department by location)
  - Gauge chart (overall engagement score)
  - Stacked area chart (headcount growth over time by dept)
  - Line chart (attrition trend, flight risk trend)
  - Donut chart (gender diversity breakdown)

DATA TABLES:
  - employees (demographics, salary, performance, engagement, flight_risk)
  - one_on_ones (meeting logs, mood, topics, sentiment)
  - goals (OKRs — title, category, status, progress, due date)
