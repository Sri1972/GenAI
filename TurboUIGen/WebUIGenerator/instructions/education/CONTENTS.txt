EDUCATION DOMAIN — CampusIQ
=============================

App Name: campus-insights
Theme: Modern edtech — approachable, colorful, student-friendly
Accent: #0EA5E9 (sky blue — openness, clarity)
Style: Bright, generous spacing, friendly typography

PAGES:
  1. Student Mosaic — Student cards with risk indicators and engagement
  2. Assignment Tracker — Submission timeline and grading overview
  3. Risk Radar — At-risk student identification with predictive scores
  4. Office Hours — Advising appointment logs and outcomes
  5. Advisor AI — AI assistant for student success queries
  6. Academic Analytics — ADVANCED CHARTS showcase (see below)

CHART TYPES EXERCISED:
  - Radar/spider chart (student wellness: academic, social, attendance, participation, financial, health)
  - Sunburst chart (course enrollment: school → department → course → section)
  - Funnel chart (student retention: Enrolled → Midterm → Finals → Pass → Graduate)
  - Box plot (GPA distribution by department)
  - Histogram (grade distribution per course)
  - Polar chart (office hour visits by day of week)
  - Bubble chart (students: credit hours vs GPA vs engagement score)
  - Gauge chart (overall retention rate)
  - Treemap (enrollment by school by major)
  - Line chart (enrollment trend over semesters)
  - Stacked bar chart (grade breakdown A/B/C/D/F by course)
  - Donut chart (student demographics breakdown)

DATA TABLES:
  - students (demographics, GPA, credits, risk_level, engagement)
  - assignments (course, submission status, grade, due dates)
  - office_hours (appointment logs, topics, outcomes, no-shows)
