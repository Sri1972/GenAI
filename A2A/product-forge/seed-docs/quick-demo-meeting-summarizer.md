# MeetingMind — AI Meeting Notes & Action Items

Upload a meeting recording (audio/video) or paste a transcript, and MeetingMind produces: a 3-bullet summary, a list of decisions made, action items with owners and due dates, and a follow-up email draft. Supports recurring meetings — it remembers prior action items and flags which ones are still open. Built as a Next.js app with a Python AI backend using Whisper for transcription and Claude for summarization. Stores meeting history in MongoDB. Team members get a weekly digest of their open action items.
